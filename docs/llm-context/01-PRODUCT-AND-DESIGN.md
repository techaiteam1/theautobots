# Product and design contract

## Product truth

The Auto Bots helps organizations transform workflows and operations into AI-powered, agentic systems. Its long-term narrative is a progression:

1. AI transformation.
2. Custom agentic systems.
3. Managed AI operations.
4. AI operating infrastructure.

Supported themes include AI agents, workflow automation, intelligent operations, custom agentic systems, and human-governed AI. Do not add unsupported client names, results, claims, statistics, testimonials, pricing, or certifications.

## Visual contract

The design is premium because it controls attention over time. It uses one focal object, one major typographic or media event at a time, large areas of untouched dark space, and long transitions with strong settling. The experience is intentionally not a conventional content grid.

Preserve these characteristics:

- A continuous near-black stage with fixed viewport layers.
- Oversized, muted, fitted sans-serif display type.
- Small navigation and labels with restrained tracking.
- Italic editorial serif for emotional headings and transitions.
- A narrow opening threshold that expands into a spatial particle scene.
- Peripheral capability words that avoid the center.
- Three media displays shown sequentially, alternating left/right/left.
- The motif on the opposite side of each desktop/tablet panel.
- Sparse centered contact treatment and quiet footer.
- Measured stillness between events; effects should not compete.

## Explicit anti-patterns

Do not introduce generic SaaS conventions unless a future product requirement explicitly overrides this contract:

- Glowing AI orbs, neon gradient wallpaper, glass cards, dashboards.
- Bento grids, feature-card rows, pricing tables, testimonial carousels.
- Dense marketing sections, badges, stock photography, fake case studies.
- Unmotivated WebGL effects or decorative animation disconnected from scroll narrative.
- A desktop layout merely stacked into equal mobile sections.

## Typography and palette

- Manrope is the self-hosted OFL sans-serif for display and body roles.
- Instrument Serif italic is the self-hosted OFL editorial face.
- Near-black is the continuous background; off-white and muted gray establish hierarchy; mint is a scarce active accent.
- Font families and all brand colors come through CSS variables. Do not scatter new brand values through components.

## IP-safe translation

The implementation independently recreates layout logic and motion character. The linked-rail motif, geometric studies, copy, logo placeholders, and source code are original. The reference site's star, award badge, project imagery, copy, proprietary fonts, and implementation are not production assets.

Maintain the minimum necessary divergence:

- Preserve function, placement, scale, visual weight, timing, and interaction role.
- Replace identifiable artistic expression and brand-specific material.
- Never move evidence from `docs/inspection/` into `public/` or application code.
